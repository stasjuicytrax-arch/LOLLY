## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla TypeScript, GSAP (ScrollTrigger, SplitText) + Lenis, static single page. Deploy: GitHub Pages (`base: '/LOLLY/'`). Decided in docs/TZ.md §4.

## Users

Booking agencies and event promoters who receive the link instead of a PDF. They are visually literate (Awwwards-level taste). In about 10 seconds they must understand who LOLLY is, how she sounds, where she has played, and how to book her.

## Product Purpose

Electronic Press Kit for LOLLY, a Myanmar-based DJ / producer (Yangon, 10+ years, first residency 2015). Success: the promoter contacts booking. A PDF version of the same design follows later (TZ §11).

## Positioning

Cinematic, loud, precise EPK of a bass-driven DJ from Myanmar with a full verifiable track record: 10 residencies, 32 venues, 8 cities, 2 countries, 7 releases, 5 videos.
